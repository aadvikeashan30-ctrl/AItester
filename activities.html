Our Process

End-to-End Value Chain

A complete workflow from procurement to consumer delivery

01

Oilseed Procurement

The union procures groundnut and sunflower seeds from multiple sources ensuring fair prices for farmers:

  • Oilseeds Growers Co-operative Societies (OGCS)
  • Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs)
  • Mill gate deliveries
  • Direct farmer procurement at MSP
500+ OGCS Partners
11 Districts
02

Oil Extraction & Processing

State-of-the-art processing facilities for maximum yield and quality:

  • Modern solvent extraction plants
  • Advanced refining technology
  • Double filtration process
  • Quality control at every stage
Processing capacity supports multiple oil varieties including sunflower, groundnut, soybean, palmolein, and rice bran
03

Modern Packaging

Advanced packaging facility operational since November 2006 in Chitradurga:

  • Automated sachet filling lines
  • PET bottle packaging
  • Bulk packaging for institutional buyers
  • Tamper-proof seal technology
6,198+ MT Packed
Multiple SKU Sizes
04

Marketing & Distribution

Creating consumer demand through strategic brand building:

  • Sungold brand - Refined Sunflower Oil (AGMARK)
  • Safal brand - Multi-variant oil range
  • Retail distribution across Karnataka
  • Institutional supply (hotels, canteens, caterers)
Sungold is the only refined sunflower oil with AGMARK certification in the market
05

Research & Development

Continuous improvement through scientific research:

  • Area Agronomic Training Center (AATC) at Haveri
  • Applied research on improved oilseed varieties
  • Seed multiplication programs
  • MoU with University of Agricultural Sciences, Bengaluru
  • Collaboration with Indian Institute of Oilseeds Research, Hyderabad
NDDB Partnership
UAS MoU Active
06

Farmer Support & Training

Comprehensive support ecosystem for oilseed growers:

  • Agronomic training and best practices
  • Quality seed distribution
  • Price stabilization through procurement policy
  • Technical advisory services
  • Market intelligence sharing
