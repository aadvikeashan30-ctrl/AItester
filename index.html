About ROGCSU

Regional Oilseeds Growers' Co-operative Union

ROGCSU Ltd., Chitradurga operates under the Karnataka Co-operative Oilseeds Growers Federation (KOF), registered on 26th October 1984. We implement the "Restructuring of Edible Oil and Oilseeds Production and Marketing in Karnataka" project, structured on the successful Anand Model.

Cooperative Model

Two-tier structure with village-level societies and state federation

Research & Development

Area Agronomic Training Center (AATC) at Haveri for applied research

Quality Assured

AGMARK certified products with international quality standards

Operation Golden Flow

Transforming oilseed production across Karnataka since 1984
