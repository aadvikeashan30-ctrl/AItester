Member Benefits

Why Join Our Cooperative?

Comprehensive support for every stage of oilseed farming

Fair Pricing

Guaranteed procurement at MSP or above, eliminating middlemen and ensuring fair returns

Quality Seeds

Access to improved oilseed varieties developed through our research programs

Training Programs

Regular agronomic training through AATC at Haveri for improved farming techniques

Easy Procurement

Multiple procurement points through OGCS, APMCs ensuring convenient selling

Market Intelligence

Price information and market trends shared with members for better decisions

Community Support

Part of a strong network of 350,000+ farmers supporting each other

How to Become a Member

1

Visit Local OGCS

Find your nearest Oilseeds Growers Co-operative Society in your village

2

Submit Application

Fill the membership form with required documents (land records, ID proof)

3

Pay Share Capital

Pay the nominal share capital amount to become a cooperative member

4

Start Benefiting

Access all member benefits including procurement, seeds, and training
