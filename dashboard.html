Service Rules & Regulations

Working Hours

Monday to Saturday: 9:30 AM - 5:30 PM | Lunch Break: 1:00 PM - 1:45 PM

Second Saturday & all Sundays are holidays.

Leave Policy

Casual Leave: 15 days/year | Earned Leave: 12 days/year | Sick Leave: 10 days/year

Maternity Leave: 180 days | Paternity Leave: 15 days

Leave must be applied 3 days in advance (except emergency). Max 3 consecutive CL days.

Conduct & Discipline

All employees must maintain decorum and follow Karnataka Co-operative Societies Act guidelines.

Unauthorized absence for more than 5 days will result in show-cause notice.

Allowances & Benefits

DA: As per Karnataka State Govt. norms | HRA: 15% of Basic | TA: ₹1,500/month

PF: 12% (Employee + Employer) | Gratuity: After 5 years of service

Promotions

Based on seniority and merit. Performance review conducted annually in March.

Minimum 3 years in current post for eligibility. DPC recommendations required.
