Our History

Karnataka Co-operative Oilseeds Growers Federation

The Karnataka Co-operative Oilseeds Growers Federation Limited (KOF), Bangalore was registered on 26th October 1984 to implement the project "Restructuring of Edible Oil and Oilseeds Production and Marketing in Karnataka".

The project was structured on the successful Anand Model of Milk Cooperative, with the objectives to increase oilseeds production, procurement and processing of oilseeds, and create the market for edible oils and its by-products under OPERATION GOLDEN FLOW.

ROGCSU Ltd., Chitradurga is one of the key beneficiaries operating under KOF, serving as the regional cooperative union for oilseed growers in the Chitradurga region.

Est. 1984

Operation Golden Flow - Transforming Karnataka's oilseed sector

Our Mission

To increase oilseed production, ensure fair procurement from farmers, process oilseeds into quality edible oils, and create sustainable markets that benefit both farmers and consumers across Karnataka.

Our Vision

To be the leading cooperative federation in India for edible oil production, making Karnataka self-sufficient in oilseeds while ensuring prosperity for every oilseed grower in the state.

Our Values

Cooperative spirit, farmer-first approach, quality assurance, sustainable practices, transparency, and commitment to community development.
